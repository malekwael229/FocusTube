FocusTube / Facebook Reels blocker

Facebook

Block Facebook Reels paths, not Facebook itself.

FocusTube limits its hard blocking to `/reel` and `/reels` routes. Normal Facebook pages stay accessible, with separate controls for a few distracting entry points.

Add to ChromeOther browsers

A narrow blocking boundary

Reels only
Strict and Warn route checks match exact `/reel` and `/reels` path boundaries, including individual Reel URLs.
Home remains open
The Facebook homepage and other non-Reels paths are not covered by the hard block.
Native media controls
After Watch Anyway, Facebook remains responsible for its own mute and playback state.
Independent mode
Facebook's mode can differ from the modes selected for the other supported sites.

Optional visual hiding

Reels navigation
Hide supported Reels list items in Facebook's navigation without removing the surrounding navigation.
Stories
Cover the recognized Stories shelf on the homepage without affecting unrelated carousel controls.
People suggestions
Hide recognized People You May Know or People You Might Know sections, including the Friends area.
Restore controls
Turn an option off and FocusTube restores the elements it hid.

How the modes behave

Strict
Blocks a Facebook Reels path immediately while leaving non-Reels pages alone.
Warn
Pauses visible page media and shows Watch Anyway before access to the current Reels surface.
Passive
Does not block Reels paths. Targeted visual hiding can still be used separately.

Why this stays specific

Facebook combines messages, groups, events, pages, and social video inside one site. Blocking the whole domain can remove tools that have nothing to do with Reels.

FocusTube treats Reels as the blocked destination and the other hiding controls as optional cleanup. That keeps the extension's behavior predictable and reduces accidental over-blocking.
