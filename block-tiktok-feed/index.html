FocusTube / TikTok feed

TikTok feed blocker

Reduce TikTok feed loops while keeping account tools available.

FocusTube recognizes TikTok home and feed routes, search, live, video, and photo paths. Its safe-page checks keep messages, upload, settings, feedback, and coin routes outside that block.

Install FocusTubeBack to overview

What changes

Feed and video paths are recognized by route

Strict Mode and a running work timer can block recognized paths. Warn Mode presents a warning before entry. Passive Mode leaves access available and does not apply route redirects.

Recognized

  • Home, /foryou, Following, Friends, Explore
  • Search and live routes
  • /video/ and /photo/ paths

Safe-page examples

  • /messages
  • /upload and /settings
  • /feedback and /coin

Modes

Choose the boundary that fits

  • StrictBlocks recognized TikTok routes.
  • WarnShows a warning before entry and offers a scoped allow action.
  • PassiveAllows normal route access without redirects or warning screens.

Local-first privacy

Configuration, timer state, and simple blocked-count statistics use browser-local storage. No analytics or telemetry endpoints are included. Review the testing guide for the project’s evidence boundaries.
