FocusTube / LinkedIn feed

LinkedIn feed hider

Keep LinkedIn for work, not endless feed scrolling.

FocusTube recognizes the LinkedIn home and /feed surfaces and places a local overlay over the detected main feed. Jobs, messaging, profiles, learning, notifications, and settings routes are outside that feed-page check.

Install FocusTubeBack to overview

What changes

Hide the recognized feed surface

On the home or /feed page, FocusTube can cover the detected main feed and the “Add to your feed” sidebar card when visual hiding is enabled. The adapter scopes its changes to recognized containers rather than hiding the whole site.

Recognized

  • LinkedIn home route
  • /feed routes
  • Detected main feed and Add to your feed card

Outside the feed check

  • /jobs and /messaging
  • Profiles, companies, schools, and learning
  • Notifications and settings

Modes

Different levels of intervention

  • StrictHides the recognized feed surface without an allow action.
  • WarnShows a warning overlay with a scoped “View Anyway” action.
  • PassiveLeaves feed access available while optional visual controls can hide selected surfaces.

Local-first and reviewable

The extension keeps settings, timer state, and simple statistics in browser-local storage. It contains no analytics or telemetry. The repository’s shows how feed classification boundaries are reviewed.
