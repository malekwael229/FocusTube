FocusTube / Instagram Reels blocker

Instagram

Block Reels and Explore, not all of Instagram.

FocusTube targets Instagram's dedicated Reels and Explore routes. Profiles, messages, notifications, and the main feed remain available.

Add to ChromeOther browsers

What FocusTube changes

Reels routes
FocusTube recognizes dedicated `/reels/` and `/reel/` paths and applies the selected platform mode.
Explore route
The `/explore/` surface is covered by the same Strict or Warn behavior.
Reels button
An optional visual control hides the Reels navigation entry without hiding Instagram Search.
Stories tray
An optional overlay covers the Stories tray while leaving the rest of the home interface usable.

What stays available

  • The main Instagram feed, including its normal controls.
  • Profiles, posts, messages, notifications, and settings.
  • Search navigation when the Reels button is hidden.
  • Stories whenever their separate hiding control is disabled.

Clear boundary: FocusTube does not claim to remove individual Reels inserted inside the main Instagram feed. That unstable feature was removed.

Strict, Warn, or Passive

Strict
Redirects away from Reels or Explore before the route becomes the session.
Warn
Shows a warning with Watch Anyway. The page-local allowance is scoped to the current Reels or Explore surface.
Passive
Leaves those routes available. The Reels navigation and Stories controls remain independently configurable.

A practical setup

  1. Choose Strict if you never need Reels, or Warn if occasional access is useful.
  2. Turn on Hide Reels Button to remove the most direct entry point.
  3. Hide Stories separately if the top-of-feed tray is the main distraction.
  4. Leave the rest of Instagram available for messages and specific accounts.
