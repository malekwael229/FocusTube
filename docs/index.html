The difference

You can need YouTube without needing Shorts.

Whole-site blockers remove the useful page along with the distraction. FocusTube targets the distracting surface instead.

  • Keep useful pagesRegular videos, search, messages, jobs and settings remain available where supported.
  • Remove common entry pointsHide buttons, shelves, stories or feeds that pull you back into scrolling.
  • Choose the frictionUse Strict, Warn or Passive mode per platform.

How it works

Pick how hard FocusTube should push back.

  • Strict
    Block immediately

    Redirect away or cover the distracting surface before you continue.

  • Warn
    Add a deliberate pause

    Show a warning first, then let you choose whether to leave or continue.

  • Passive
    Browse normally

    Do not block the route. Visual hiding can still remove selected distracting entry points.

Privacy

Your settings stay in your browser.

FocusTube is designed to work locally. There is no account system and no project-controlled backend.

  • No analyticsNo analytics SDKs or usage dashboards in the extension.
  • No trackingNo tracking pixels or browsing-history collection.
  • No accountsNo sign-up, login or profile system.
  • No project backendNo settings or browsing data sent to a FocusTube server.
  • Local storagePreferences, timer state and stats use browser-local extension storage.
  • User-opened linksExternal GitHub or store pages open only when you choose them.
