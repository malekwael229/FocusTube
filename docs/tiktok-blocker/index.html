FocusTube / TikTok

TikTok distraction control

Add friction to TikTok without blocking every utility page.

FocusTube targets common feed and video surfaces so you can reduce casual scrolling while keeping safer utility areas available where supported.

Add to ChromeFirefoxEdge

What FocusTube changes

Target the distracting surface.

Feed surfaces
Common feed routes can be blocked in Strict or Warn mode.
Video routes
Supported video pages can be intercepted before you continue.
Live and photo surfaces
Common live and photo routes are included in TikTok blocking logic.
Utility areas
Messages and settings are examples of areas FocusTube is designed to leave available.

What stays available

Keep the useful part of TikTok.

FocusTube is not intended to make TikTok completely inaccessible. The goal is to target high-distraction surfaces while preserving supported utility areas.

TikTok route behavior changes over time. The extension uses site-specific rules rather than a generic full-domain block.

Blocking modes

Choose the amount of friction.

Strict blocks the targeted surface immediately. Warn shows an interstitial first. Passive keeps browsing normal while visual hiding controls can still remove selected UI.

Settings, timer state and local estimates remain in browser-local extension storage. FocusTube does not use analytics, tracking, accounts or a project-controlled backend.
