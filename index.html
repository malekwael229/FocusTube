Choose your level of friction

Strict, Warn, or Passive

Modes are selected per platform. A focus timer can also enforce blocking while a work session is active.

  • StrictRedirects supported distracting routes or shows a blocking overlay where that platform uses an in-page surface.
  • WarnShows a warning before entering supported content, with a scoped allow action where implemented.
  • PassiveLeaves direct access available while optional visual hiding removes selected entry points and surfaces.

Local by design

Useful controls without a tracking layer

FocusTube stores settings, timer state, and simple blocked-count statistics in browser-local storage. The extension source contains no analytics or telemetry endpoints and exposes no project-controlled backend.

  • MIT licensedRead the source and license on GitHub.
  • Native browser localizationEight packaged catalogs, including Arabic RTL metadata for extension-owned UI.
