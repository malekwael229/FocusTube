FocusTube / LinkedIn

LinkedIn distraction control

Hide the LinkedIn feed without losing LinkedIn.

Use LinkedIn for jobs, messages and profiles without putting the main feed in front of you every time you open the site.

Add to ChromeFirefoxEdge

What FocusTube changes

Target the distracting surface.

Main feed
FocusTube can hide the primary LinkedIn feed surface.
Add to your feed
The Add to your feed sidebar card can be hidden.
Passive mode
You can keep normal browsing while using only the visual hiding controls.
Strict or Warn
Use stronger blocking modes where supported if visual hiding is not enough.

What stays available

Keep the useful part of LinkedIn.

Jobs, messages, profiles and other LinkedIn areas are not the target of the feed-hiding controls described here.

LinkedIn is useful for work and recruiting, which is exactly why selective hiding is often more practical than blocking the entire domain.

Blocking modes

Choose the amount of friction.

Strict blocks the targeted surface immediately. Warn shows an interstitial first. Passive keeps browsing normal while visual hiding controls can still remove selected UI.

Settings, timer state and local estimates remain in browser-local extension storage. FocusTube does not use analytics, tracking, accounts or a project-controlled backend.
