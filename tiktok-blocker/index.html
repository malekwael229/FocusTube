FocusTube / TikTok blocker

TikTok

Put a boundary around TikTok's scrolling routes.

FocusTube recognizes the common TikTok paths built around feeds, search, live content, photos, and videos, including locale-prefixed URLs.

Add to ChromeOther browsers

Routes FocusTube can block

  • The root For You feed and `/foryou` routes.
  • Following, Friends, Explore, and Search routes.
  • Live pages and individual video or photo routes.
  • Equivalent locale-prefixed paths such as `/fr` or `/en-US`.

TikTok changes route shapes over time. FocusTube normalizes the locale prefix before checking the supported path.

Utility pages stay available

Messages
Paths under `/messages` are treated as safe.
Uploads
The upload flow remains available for people who publish intentionally.
Settings
Settings, feedback, and supported coin pages are not part of the blocked feed set.
Per-platform choice
TikTok has its own mode, separate from YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn.

Choose the interruption

Strict
Covers a supported TikTok distraction route and offers a route back to Messages.
Warn
Shows a decision screen. Watch Anyway temporarily allows that specific route category.
Passive
Removes the intervention and leaves TikTok navigation unchanged.

When this is useful

TikTok is often opened for one message, upload, or specific link and then turns into an open-ended feed session. FocusTube keeps the utility routes accessible while adding friction to the paths designed around repeated content.

Warn mode fits occasional intentional viewing. Strict mode fits study or work periods where the feed has no immediate purpose.
