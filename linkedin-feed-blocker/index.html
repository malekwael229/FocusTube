FocusTube / LinkedIn feed blocker

LinkedIn

Use LinkedIn without opening the main feed.

FocusTube covers LinkedIn's feed surface and the Add to your feed sidebar card while leaving work-focused destinations available.

Add to ChromeOther browsers

What FocusTube changes

Main feed
On LinkedIn's root and `/feed` pages, FocusTube can place a local overlay over the feed column.
Add to your feed
A separate option covers the recognized recommendation card in the sidebar.
Warn access
Warn mode adds a dismissible decision before the feed becomes visible.
Focus timer
An active work timer applies the stricter feed treatment until the session ends.

Work pages stay available

  • Messages and notifications.
  • Jobs, My Network, and LinkedIn Learning.
  • Personal profiles, company pages, and school pages.
  • Settings and account controls.

FocusTube covers the feed column in the page. It does not disable LinkedIn or replace its navigation.

Modes fit the feed

Strict
Covers the main feed without a dismiss button.
Warn
Covers the feed with a choice to reveal it for the current session.
Passive
Leaves the feed visible. The separate Add to your feed control can still be configured.

Designed for task-based visits

LinkedIn is useful when you arrive with a specific task: reply to a message, check a job, update a profile, or visit a company page. The main feed is not required for any of those jobs.

FocusTube keeps those destinations accessible and makes the feed a deliberate choice instead of the default landing surface.
